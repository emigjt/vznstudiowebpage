    About

    One studio for the sound and the picture.

    VZN Studios brings audio and visual production under one roof. Instead of handing a project between a producer, an engineer, a videographer and a designer who never talk to each other, we build the whole thing together — so the record, the video and the artwork all feel like the same idea.

    We work with artists, brands and creators who care about how their work looks and sounds, from a single track to a full release campaign.

    • Vision first. Every project starts with the story, not the gear.
    • In-house. Audio and visual teams share one creative direction.
    • Release-ready. Deliverables formatted for streaming, social and broadcast.

    Services

    What we make

    Audio

    • Music Production

      Beats, arrangements and full productions built around your sound, from demo to finished record.

    • Recording

      Vocal and instrument tracking with direction to get the take that carries the song.

    • Mixing & Mastering

      Balanced, loud and clear mixes, mastered for streaming platforms, radio and vinyl.

    • Sound Design & Scoring

      Original music, foley and sonic branding for film, ads, games and content.

    • Creative Direction & Branding

    Process

    How a project runs

    1. 01

      Discover

      We talk through the idea, references, audience and timeline, then send a scope and quote.

    2. 02

      Create

      Production, shoots and sessions — with check-ins so you hear and see progress as it happens.

    3. 03

      Refine

      Revision rounds on mixes, edits and artwork until every piece feels finished.

    4. 04

      Release

      Final masters and exports delivered in every format you need for launch.
